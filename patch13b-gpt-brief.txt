SECTION 4 — NEW ITEMS FOR 13B (FROM RUN DATA)
---------------------------------------------------------------

P1 — Hard crisis gate (Runs 7, 9)
  When E<=5 OR H<=5:
    - All movement must be goal-directed (toward food/water target),
      escape, or oscillation-break output.
    - Random or exploratory movement is NOT allowed.
    - Eat/drink must override movement when available on current tile.
  Implementation: flag at top of chooseIntelligentBotAction(), applied
  before any movement selection that isn't already goal-anchored.

P2 — Resource anchor (Run 8)
  When: safe multi-portion food present AND (E<=30 OR H<=15 OR F<=15)
    - Eat BEFORE considering movement.
    - Do not leave food tile until: all portions consumed OR threat appears.
  Implementation: check in intelligentFindSafeEat() or pre-movement gate
  using current tile encounter data.

P3 — Water→food handoff tightened (Run 2 — A1 gap)
  Current: A1 blocks drink at H>=65 AND E<=10. NOT sufficient.
  Run 2 died at H=52 with E=0, still drinking.
  Required: if E=0 AND H>=40 AND poisoned, block drink-water AND groom,
  force seek_food goal.
  Also: block groom in criticalResource path when E<=10 (groom seen at
  t356 with E=0, H=54, while poisoned).

P4 — Clay validity filter (Run 9)
  Clay bank ONLY allowed when player.poisoned > 0.
  Otherwise excluded from intelligentFindSafeEat() results entirely.
  Implementation: single guard in resource selection, check remedy type.

P5 — Oscillation blacklist duration (Runs 6, 8, 9)
  Current: blacklist 3 turns. Still seeing loops.
  Increase to 6 turns.
  Ensure perpendicular escape: if N/S are blacklisted, force E or W output
  (not just "filter and hope").

P6 — Escape stickiness (Runs 3, 4, 11)
  Add escapeTimer to botState.memory (init 0).
  On any EMERGENCY_ESCAPE turn involving aerial predator: escapeTimer = 3.
  In determineIntelligentBotMode(): if escapeTimer > 0 and no new threat,
  decrement and return RECOVER (not EXPLORE).
  EMERGENCY_ESCAPE must still override escapeTimer if new threat appears.
